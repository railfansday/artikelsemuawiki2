Topic Description: Upcoming superhero film

The Batman: Part II is an upcoming American superhero film directed by Matt Reeves from a screenplay he wrote with Mattson Tomlin. Based on the DC Comics character Batman, it is the sequel to The Batman (2022).

Robert Pattinson stars as Bruce Wayne / Batman alongside Jeffrey Wright, Andy Serkis, Colin Farrell, Sebastian Stan, Scarlett Johansson, and Charles Dance. Set during the winter, Batman investigates further corruption after the flooding of Gotham City.

Warner Bros. Pictures will distribute the film, which Reeves also produces with Lynn Harris and Dylan Clark, alongside James Gunn and Peter Safran of DC Studios.
