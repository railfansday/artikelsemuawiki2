Topic Description: Association football club in Bruges, Belgium

Club Brugge Koninklijke Voetbalvereniging, known simply as Club Brugge, is a Belgian professional football club based in Bruges, Belgium. It was founded in 1891 and its home ground is the Jan Breydel Stadium, which has a capacity of 29,042.

They play in the Belgian Pro League, the top domestic league in Belgian football.
