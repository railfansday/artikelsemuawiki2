Topic Description: Association football club in England

Hull City Association Football Club is a professional association football club based in Kingston upon Hull, East Riding of Yorkshire, England. They compete in the Premier League, the top tier of English football.

Hull play their home games at the MKM Stadium, after moving from Boothferry Park in 2002. The club's traditional home colours are black and amber, often featuring in a striped design on the shirt, hence their nickname, the Tigers.

Hull also contest the Humber derby with both Grimsby Town and Scunthorpe United.
