Topic Description: 2026 Indian film by Deepak Kumar Mishra

The Vvaan: Force of the Forrest is a 2026 Indian Hindi-language fantasy thriller film directed by Deepak Kumar Mishra and produced by Shobha Kapoor, Ekta Kapoor, Niraj Kothari, and Arunabh Kumar under Balaji Motion Pictures, TVF Motion Pictures and 11:11 Productions. The film stars Sidharth Malhotra in a dual role as Rishi Kumar and Veer, alongside Tamannaah Bhatia, Anup Soni, Sunil Grover, Shweta Tiwari and Maniesh Paul.

It is inspired by Van Devi Temple and its folklore. The film was theatrically released on 25 September 2026.
