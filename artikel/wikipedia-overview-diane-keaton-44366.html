Topic Description: American actress (1946–2025)

Diane Keaton was an American actress. Her career spanned more than five decades, during which she rose to prominence in the New Hollywood movement.

She collaborated frequently with Woody Allen, appearing in eight of his films. Keaton's accolades include an Academy Award, a BAFTA Award, and two Golden Globe Awards, along with nominations for two Emmy Awards and a Tony Award.

She was honored with the Film at Lincoln Center Gala Tribute in 2007 and the AFI Life Achievement Award in 2017.
