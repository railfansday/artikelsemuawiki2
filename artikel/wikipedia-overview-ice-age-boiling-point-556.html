Topic Description: Upcoming animated film

Ice Age: Boiling Point is an upcoming American animated adventure comedy film directed by John C. Donkin.

Produced by 20th Century Animation, it is the sixth main installment in the Ice Age film series following Ice Age: Collision Course (2016), and the seventh installment overall. The film features Ray Romano, John Leguizamo, Denis Leary, Simon Pegg, and Queen Latifah reprising their roles from the previous films.

It will be the first theatrical Ice Age film not produced by Blue Sky Studios, which closed in April 2021.
