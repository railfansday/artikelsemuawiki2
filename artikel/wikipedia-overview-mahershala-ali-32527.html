Topic Description: American actor (born 1974)

Mahershala Ali is an American actor. He has received multiple accolades, including two Academy Awards, a BAFTA Award, a Golden Globe Award, and a Primetime Emmy Award.

Films in which he has appeared have grossed over $3.3 billion worldwide. In 2020, The New York Times ranked him among the 25 greatest actors of the 21st century.

Time magazine named him one of the 100 most influential people in the world in 2019.
