Topic Description: American drama television series

The Hunting Wives is an American drama television series based on the novel of the same name by May Cobb. It stars Brittany Snow, Malin Åkerman, Evan Jonigkeit, Katie Lowes, George Ferrier, Dermot Mulroney, Jaime Ray Newman, and Chrissy Metz.

The series premiered on Netflix on July 21, 2025. In September 2025, the series was renewed for a second season which premiered on November 26, 2026.

In October 2026, ahead of the second season premiere, the series was renewed for a third season.
