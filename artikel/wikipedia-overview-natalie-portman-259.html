Topic Description: Israeli and American actress (born 1981)

Natalie Hershlag, known professionally as Natalie Portman, is an actress and filmmaker with dual Israeli and American citizenship. She has had a screen career from her teenage years and has starred in various blockbusters and independent films.

Her accolades include an Academy Award, a BAFTA Award, and two Golden Globe Awards.
