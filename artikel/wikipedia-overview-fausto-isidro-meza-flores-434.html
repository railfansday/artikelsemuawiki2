Topic Description: Mexican drug trafficker

Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, also known as "El Chapo Isidro", is a Mexican drug lord and leader of Los Mazatlecos. He held a high rank in the Beltrán Leyva Cartel and was the right-hand man of Alfredo Beltrán Leyva before Beltrán Leyva was incarcerated in the United States.

On February 4, 2025, he was added to the FBI Ten Most Wanted Fugitives list. He was captured during a Mexican military operation in Guasave on November 20, 2026, sparking retaliatory cartel violence in the area.
