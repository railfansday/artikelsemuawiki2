Topic Description: 2026 film by Peter Farrelly

I Play Rocky is a 2026 American biographical drama film directed by Peter Farrelly and written by Peter Gamble. It stars Anthony Ippolito, AnnaSophia Robb, Toby Kebbell, P.

J. Byrne, Jay Duplass, Matt Dillon, Stephan James, and Tracy Letts.

In the film, Sylvester Stallone (Ippolito) battles tumultuous production to portray the titular character of his self-written film, Rocky (1976).
